About

Benz-Action was forged on Moroccan sets, where the world comes to shoot its most ambitious action.

Founded in 2010 by Youness Benzakour, BENZ-ACTION is headquartered in Casablanca, Morocco, and works across Morocco, the Maghreb and international productions.

For over 15 years our stunt performers, riggers, pyrotechnicians and coordinators have served features, series and commercials — from Ouarzazate's studios to international locations. We speak the language of directors, line producers and safety officers alike.

Our method never changes: analyze the script, engineer the risk out, rehearse until it's muscle memory, then deliver a spectacular take. Safety is not a constraint — it's what makes the impossible repeatable.
